🧭 Market Themes

1. US-Iran Peace Deal — The Market's Biggest Macro Catalyst. Trump called a deal "very possible" and Iran is reviewing the US framework proposal. Oil has fallen sharply — WTI below $100 intraday — which would be massively disinflationary if a deal closes. A confirmed deal could send oil to $70–80, cut inflation by 1–2%, and give the Fed room to cut rates. Every risk asset benefits. Reuters, The Guardian

2. Agentic AI — The New CPU Supercycle. AMD's earnings validated a thesis that agentic AI workloads require massive CPU capacity, not just GPUs. Lisa Su revised the server CPU market growth forecast to 35%+ annually, topping $120B by decade-end. This is a structural shift that benefits AMD, Intel, and indirectly NVDA. The AI trade is broadening beyond pure GPU plays. CNBC

3. Valuation vs. Fundamentals Tension. PLTR beat on every metric — 85% revenue growth, 133% US commercial growth — yet sold off because it trades at 154x P/E. ARM beat and reversed. The market is increasingly punishing perfection-priced stocks even on strong results. This is a late-cycle earnings dynamic: the bar is set by valuation, not just fundamentals. 247WallSt

4. Oil as the Inflation Wildcard. WTI below $100 is a significant development — it's the first time since the Hormuz crisis began. If a deal closes and the strait reopens, oil could fall another 20–30%, dramatically changing the inflation and rate outlook. The Fed is watching closely. Energy stocks are the obvious loser; everything else wins. CNBC

5. Record Highs, Digestion Mode. The S&P 500 closed at 7,365 Tuesday — up 15%+ in April and early May. Today is a consolidation session. Breadth is narrowing, ARM is dragging tech, and investors are waiting for the Iran deal catalyst before the next leg. The risk is a "deal falls through" shock that sends oil back above $110. Yahoo Finance
