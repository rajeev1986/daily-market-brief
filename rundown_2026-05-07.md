## 6. Market Themes

**1. US-Iran Peace Deal — The Market's Biggest Macro Catalyst.** Trump called a deal "very possible" and Iran is reviewing the US framework proposal. Oil has fallen sharply — WTI below $100 intraday — which would be massively disinflationary if a deal closes. A confirmed deal could send oil to $70–80, cut inflation by 1–2%, and give the Fed room to cut rates. Every risk asset benefits. [Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/us-oil-prices-rebound-investors-assess-prospects-middle-east-peace-deal-2026-05-06/), [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/world/live/2026/may/07/middle-east-crisis-iran-trump-us-negotiations-deal-war-peace-proposal-hormuz-latest-news-updates)

**2. Agentic AI — The New CPU Supercycle.** AMD's earnings validated a thesis that agentic AI workloads require massive CPU capacity, not just GPUs. Lisa Su revised the server CPU market growth forecast to 35%+ annually, topping $120B by decade-end. This is a structural shift that benefits AMD, Intel, and indirectly NVDA. The AI trade is broadening beyond pure GPU plays. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/06/amd-lisa-su-stock-forecast-earnings.html)

**3. Valuation vs. Fundamentals Tension.** PLTR beat on every metric — 85% revenue growth, 133% US commercial growth — yet sold off because it trades at 154x P/E. ARM beat and reversed. The market is increasingly punishing perfection-priced stocks even on strong results. This is a late-cycle earnings dynamic: the bar is set by valuation, not just fundamentals. [247WallSt](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/05/06/nvidia-is-a-buy-amd-is-a-hold-and-palantir-is-a-sell-here-is-the-math-behind-each-call/)

**4. Oil as the Inflation Wildcard.** WTI below $100 is a significant development — it's the first time since the Hormuz crisis began. If a deal closes and the strait reopens, oil could fall another 20–30%, dramatically changing the inflation and rate outlook. The Fed is watching closely. Energy stocks are the obvious loser; everything else wins. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/07/oil-prices-today-trump-iran-strait-of-hormuz-us-crude-brent-.html)

**5. Record Highs, Digestion Mode.** The S&P 500 closed at 7,365 Tuesday — up 15%+ in April and early May. Today is a consolidation session. Breadth is narrowing, ARM is dragging tech, and investors are waiting for the Iran deal catalyst before the next leg. The risk is a "deal falls through" shock that sends oil back above $110. [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/live/stock-market-today-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-futures-tread-water-in-wait-for-iran-us-deal-update-231446298.html)
